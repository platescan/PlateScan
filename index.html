    How XP, bosses and gold work

    Every meal logged earns 10 XP (up to 5 a day), plus 5 more when you scan it. Hitting protein is 25, landing within 10% of your calorie target (2+ meals) is 25, a weigh-in is 10, your water goal is 10, and back-to-back days add 2 XP per streak day (up to 20).

    A new boss stage opens every 2 levels once you’ve beaten the one before. Damage them with squats, push-ups (×1.5 damage) or pull-ups (×3). Your camera checks each rep, and only full-range reps count. A perfect rep is a critical hit. If you stop repping for too long, the boss attacks and you lose a heart. Each new boss has 12% more HP.

    Bosses drop XP and gold. Harder difficulty pays more (up to ×3.5). Replays pay 40%, once per boss per day. Gold also comes from your daily chest and quick workouts. Spend it on XP potions, shields, gloves and streak freezes.

    How points work (and why it’s fair)

    Everyone earns points against their own targets, so size, goal and fitness level don’t matter. Each day: 2+ meals logged (10), protein at 90% of your target (10), calories within 10% of your target (10), your water goal (5), and 10+ good reps in a workout or boss fight (10). That’s 45 a day at most, and one category counts double each week for everyone. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Friends see your points, level and streak. Food, calories and weight stay private unless you choose to share them in Locker → Profile.

    Home & Lock Screen widget

    Choose what it shows. Tap in the order you want; number 1 is shown biggest.

    Style
    Preview

    Changes reach your widget within about 15 minutes. No need to copy the script again.

    How to add it
    1. Install Scriptable (free) from the App Store.
    2. Tap Copy widget script above.
    3. Open Scriptable, tap +, paste, and name the script PlateScan. Already have it? Open it, select all, and paste over it once to get the new options.
    4. Home Screen: press and hold an empty spot → Edit → Add Widget → Scriptable → small, medium or large → Add. Tap the widget, set Script to PlateScan.
    5. Lock Screen: press and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget area → Scriptable. Tap the new widget and set Script to PlateScan.
    6. Make one widget different: in its settings, type into Parameter, for example quests or kcal,water. Options: streak, quests, kcal, protein, water, level.

    Smart answers (optional)

    Your coach already answers common questions for free, privately, on your phone. Add a free Google AI key and it can answer almost anything about your data.

    How to get a free key
    1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
    2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
    3. Paste it above and tap Save and test.

    Your key is saved only on this phone. With smart answers on, your question and a summary of your PlateScan data go to Google’s Gemini API. On Google’s free tier, Google may use them to improve its products. Safety and crisis replies always come from the built-in coach.
